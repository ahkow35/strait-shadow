You command a Type 218SG-style submarine. Formidable-class frigates and their Seahawk helicopters hunt you. Listen, stay quiet, find a firing position, shoot, and slip away.

How to play

Each mission states its goal at the top left. Your hull has three segments: a torpedo hit costs one, sometimes two, and running aground costs one. A frigate takes two torpedo hits.

  • Listen. The ring shows where sounds come from: the top of the ring is your bow. A bigger dot is a louder, closer contact. You hear less when you go fast, and nothing straight behind you.
  • Stay quiet. Speed makes noise, steeply above Half. Going fast near the surface is louder still. Silent running halves your noise but holds you at Slow and stops reloading. Quiet is strong, not perfect: an active ping, a dipping sonar or a sonobuoy close by can still find you.
  • Use the layer. The dashed line on the depth gauge is a layer of colder water. Below it the frigate's hull sonar hears you poorly. The helicopter lowers its sonar through the layer, so it still can.
  • Plot. The map draws each sound as a bearing line. Cross the line of sight and the lines firm up into a position: the ellipse shows how unsure it still is. A ping or a look through the periscope gives the exact place at once, and gives you away. Drag the map to look around; Centre brings it back to your boat. The small strip on the map is the bearing history: every sound leaves a trace, newest at the top, so a trace that slides sideways is a contact whose bearing is changing.
  • Shoot. Point the bow at the target and fire. The torpedo trails a wire: steer it left or right until it starts homing. A hard turn, more than Half speed, or a long run breaks the wire. The frigate drops noisemakers; a torpedo still on its wire is rarely fooled.
  • Slip away. When a torpedo is in the water, release a noisemaker, turn away and go deep and quiet. You carry four (a game value).
  • Stars. Each mission gives up to three stars, and the title screen says what earns them. A mission opens once the one before it has a star. Free Patrol is always open: frigates keep coming, and the score is kept.
